The Framework

Signal + Proof

Everything AI needs to confidently recommend your brand falls into two categories. This is the system that builds both.

Pillar One

Signal

Clarity + Structure

  • Scannable product descriptions structured for decisions, not impressions
  • FAQ blocks that answer the questions buyers actually ask AI
  • Comparison pages that win "Brand A vs Brand B" queries
  • Best-for pages that capture specific use-case searches
  • Structured spec sheets that are impossible to misread
Pillar Two

Proof

Trust + Credibility

  • Reviews with enough detail for AI to cite as real evidence
  • Plain-English shipping, returns, and warranty policies
  • Specific, prominent guarantees that remove purchase risk
  • Social proof formats AI can read, parse, and quote
  • Authority signals: press, certifications, founder credibility
